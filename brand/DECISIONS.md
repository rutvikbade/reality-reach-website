## 2026-10-06 — the house brand and its site *(owner)*

- **A new corporate site, built from scratch.** The live realityreach.com is "merely a placeholder for legal and app review purposes"; the earlier attempt is "not quite hitting the spot". The new site is storytelling with "minimal copy ofcourse but high signal, to claim our technological authority".
- **Purpose is not conversion.** "it is something someone looks up when they want to look into us, or wanting to deal with us, and they should realise we are superior trustworthy authority and they just very strongly desire that we be on their side. it should open up conversations, opportunities, compound brand equity."
- **Positioning by feeling.** Reality Reach is the "right" intelligence partner for the next decade: demographics, technology, AI, acceleration, entropy. "these are just my emotions, i am not asking you to be explicit with it."
- **The house and its products.** "RR is the parent, capwatch is specific product brand." "the brand, guidelines, voice, etc are almost similar to capwatch". "we are very likely adopting much of the things from capwatch, we are not reinventing many things." Same fonts and hierarchy as capwatch.in.
- **The look.**
  - Light mode, "white background, but blueprint like microgrid (not blue color)". "mainly white and black".
  - Full-viewport dark sections are allowed: the capwatch.in hero showcasing the product with App Store, Google Play and Microsoft Store badges.
  - "boundary lines or edges or L corners", done properly this time.
- **The signature.** ASCII animation, as SVG watch drawings were for capwatch.in: scroll-based, morphing, telling the story. The first reference is the *Wish You Were Here* handshake (emotion only). It can morph into a globe, or into cranes and construction: co-building, new heights, infrastructure, national or private, digital or physical.
- **Disclosure.**
  - Hardware is never named, and "maybe not even the full concept".
  - Antaxial (one custom deployment, not productised): "maybe we can mention it i guess but not emphasise?" (◐ hedged; recorded as mention-once in `01` §4).
  - CapWatch is the only product served to the public.
- **Constraints.**
  - Responsive with no compromises (web, mobile, tablet, iPhone Duo).
  - "hard avoid external dependencies".
  - "we do have to stay legally intact".
  - A $500k feel.
  - No autocommit; work only inside RR-Website.
- **A brand department** for Reality Reach "if needed", with "proper housing of assets or collateral". This folder.
- **The repo.** RR-Website stays where it is, as a clean repo the owner can push to replace the current code. The root is `README.md`, `.gitignore`, `site/` and `brand/`. The old site files are deleted; they remain in git history (`dfcda16`). The brand set stays lean: "don't overcomplicate, no wasted effort".
