# 04 — Voice

> **Version** 0.2 · **Date** 2026-10-06 · **Status** DRAFT ◐. Inherits CapWatch's register from intracalc `brand/seed/voice.md` and `brand/guidelines/04-VOICE-STANDARDS.md`. Adapted for the house from the owner's direction of 2026-10-06.

## 1. The register, inherited

The register is the same as CapWatch's: "cold yet organic Swiss business" (`seed/voice.md`). Six attributes carry over verbatim from CapWatch `04`:
- **Declarative:** present tense, statements, not announcements.
- **Specific:** the concrete over the abstract.
- **Restrained with rhythm:** a short statement, then a longer specifying clause.
- **Impersonal:** no narrator, no addressed reader in headlines.
- **Honest:** no claim the house cannot substantiate.
- **Crafted:** one theatre; the rest is utility.

Four adaptations for the house:
- **The reader is the counterpart, not the owner.** They are a founder, board member, investor, officer, senior hire or partner, deciding whether to deal with Reality Reach. Speak to their decade, not their quarter.
- **The subject is the house or the work.** "Reality Reach builds…" is preferred to "We build…". *We* appears only where hiding it would blur who is accountable (rare; the legal page uses the entity's name instead).
- **Second person:** never in a headline. Imperatives without pronouns are allowed: "Begin a conversation."
- **Indian English:** organisation, recognised, programme.

## 2. Positioning ◐

- **Line:** *Intelligence partners for the decade ahead.*
  - *Intelligence* = computation and judgement, the discipline.
  - *Partners* = the handshake (FIG. 01), the relationship.
  - *The decade ahead* = the horizon, the reason to choose now.
- **Identity sentence:** *Software, systems and infrastructure for the organisations that will shape what comes next.*

## 3. The thesis: felt in the headlines, proven in the readings

Over the next ten years two forces arrive at once: India's demographic window (open 2013–2050, UN WPP 2024) and artificial intelligence (almost 40 percent of employment worldwide exposed to it, IMF 2024; GPT-3.5-level inference over 280 times cheaper in under two years, Stanford AI Index 2025). Most institutions are not built for both. The demand of growth this decade is not more software but intelligence built into how an organisation actually runs, while the window is open. The organisations that lead will be the ones with the right partner beside them. Reality Reach is that partner.

The reader should finish wanting Reality Reach on their side. Nothing on the page says so. ✅ *(owner, 2026-10-06: "i am not asking you to be explicit with it")*

**Carried by:**
- the figures (`09`) and the spine: partnership → the decade → the work (three chapters);
- one line per chapter;
- the Readings section, which proves the stakes with cited figures (publisher, year, title, link, accessed date);
- precision, restraint, discretion and craft. "Strongest", "most enlightened", "winning" are felt, never written (ASCI: no unsubstantiated superlatives).

**Never carried by:** an uncited statistic, a list of trends, or the vocabulary of the thesis itself. Never say *demographic dividend*, *disruption*, *AI revolution*, *future-proof*, *entropy*, *winning side*, *Viksit*, or a year-target slogan. *Artificial intelligence* appears as a plain noun, beside a cited fact.

**Credible, not boastful:** the side is chosen by the reader ("for organisations that intend to lead what comes next") and the promise is duration ("taken through the decade"), never superiority. *Discretion, by default* also explains, without apology, why so little work is shown (`01` §4).

## 5. Patterns

- **Headline:** one declarative, nine words or fewer, ending in a full stop. A category name ("Automated Capital Governance") takes no full stop.
- **Lead or body:** one or two sentences, each 28 words or fewer.
- **Eyebrows and captions:** DM Mono, sentence case. FIG. labels are uppercase (`FIG. 02 — THE DECADE`).
- **Numbers:** numerals. Dates as "6 October 2026"; "2022" stands alone.
- **Bullets:** end with full stops (CapWatch `seed/voice.md`).

## 6. Words

| Use | build, partner, decade, organisation, systems, infrastructure, intelligence (as a discipline), engineering, measured, discretion, endure |
|---|---|
| **Avoid** | solutions, leverage, empower, seamless, transform, unlock, journey, ecosystem, synergy, innovative or innovating, cutting-edge, next-gen, world-class, best-in-class, revolutionary, disrupt, democratise, stealth, "possibilities" |
| **Never** | superlatives (incl. strongest, most enlightened, winning side); exclamation marks; emoji; *AI-powered* / *AI-driven* (AI is allowed only as a plain noun, where specific); *IoT*, *hardware*, or any undisclosed product (`01` §4); *family* (inherited); *Intracalc*; a client's name without consent; headcount, client count, revenue, or any figure the house cannot substantiate; a credential that does not yet exist |

## 7. Tone by context

| Context | Second person | Note |
|---|---|---|
| Website | Never in headlines; pronoun-free imperatives allowed | One line per idea; the figure carries the feeling. |
| Deck | Never on slides; the speaker may say *you* | One figure and one line per slide. |
| LinkedIn | Sparingly | The work, the craft, the decade. Never a launch voice. |
| Paper (quotation, invoice, letter) | Never | The legal person's register: entity block, facts (CapWatch `06` P10a). |
| Email | In the greeting only | From support@ until a house address is ruled (`01` §7). |
